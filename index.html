💳 Payment ka kya tarika hai? +
Hum sirf advance online payment accept karte hain — Cash on Delivery available nahi hai. Teen options hain: Bank Transfer (UBL), EasyPaisa, ya JazzCash. Payment karne ke baad screenshot upload karna hoga order form mein. Payment confirm hone ke baad hi order process hoga.
🇵🇰 Kya saare phones PTA Approved hain? +
Hamare paas PTA Approved, JV (Joint Venture), aur Non-PTA — teeno options available hain. Har product card pe clearly likha hota hai. PTA Approved phones mein koi network ya feature issue nahi hoti Pakistan mein. Agar aap PTA specifically chahte hain to filter use karen ya WhatsApp pe confirm karen.
🚚 Delivery aur Pickup ka kya process hai? +
📍 Rawalpindi / Islamabad: Website pe order karo, payment bhejo (Bank / EasyPaisa / JazzCash), screenshot upload karo — phir same day store se pick up karo. Address: Shop #26, 5th Floor, Singapore Plaza, Saddar, Rawalpindi.

🚚 Baaki Pakistan: Payment confirm hone ke baad 2-4 business days mein TCS/Leopards ke zariye courier kiya jata hai. Tracking number WhatsApp pe milega.
🛡️ Kya warranty milti hai? +
Haan! Saare brand new phones pe manufacturer warranty hoti hai. Used phones pe ZP Mobile Zone ki 1 month in-store warranty hoti hai. Agar koi hardware issue aaye, hum warranty claim mein help karte hain. Accessories pe 7-day replacement guarantee hai.
🔄 Kya purana phone trade-in kar sakte hain? +
Bilkul! Apna purana phone la ke naya upgrade lo. Hum Rawalpindi mein best trade-in rates dete hain. WhatsApp pe apne phone ki model, condition, aur photos bhejein — hum foran price bata denge.
✅ Phones genuine hain — kaise verify karein? +
Har phone ka IMEI number PTA website pe verify kar sakte hain. Saath hi box, cable, aur original accessories saath aate hain. Agar koi doubt ho to store visit karein — hum aapke samne IMEI verify karenge. ZP Mobile Zone mein koi fake product nahi milta — yeh hamara guarantee hai.
